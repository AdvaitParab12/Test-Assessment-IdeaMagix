Eligibility For Treatment.

Any individual who has tried diet and exercise but failed to lose weight is considered as a good candidate for gastric bypass. Although gastric bypass aids in weight loss, it is not an appropriate option for everyone. Based on the overall health and certain medical guidelines, the doctor will decide whether you are eligible for gastric bypass or not.

In general, gastric bypass could be a good option if:

  • The BMI (body mass index) is 40 or more
  • Your BMI is 30 or more with severe weight-related health conditions like type-2 diabetes and high blood pressure
  • You are willing to make permanent changes in the lifestyle

About Treatment.

Gastric bypass is a surgical procedure that helps people lose weight by making changes to the stomach and digestive system. The procedure reduces the amount of food that can be consumed and changes the way nutrients are absorbed.

The treatment is performed by experienced medical professionals after a complete evaluation of the patient's health and medical history.

  • The procedure helps reduce excess body weight
  • It may improve weight-related health conditions
  • Long-term lifestyle changes are required

Post-Treatment Care.

After treatment, patients need to follow the instructions provided by their healthcare team. Proper care helps support recovery and reduces the risk of complications.

Important post-treatment instructions include:

  • Follow the recommended diet plan
  • Take prescribed medicines as instructed
  • Attend scheduled follow-up appointments
  • Gradually return to normal physical activities

Patient Recovery Tips.

Recovery can be different for every patient. Following a healthy routine and maintaining regular communication with the medical team can help make the recovery process smoother.

Some useful recovery tips include:

  • Drink enough water throughout the day
  • Follow your prescribed meal schedule
  • Get enough rest and sleep
  • Follow the recommended exercise routine
